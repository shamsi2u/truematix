Find the fakes selling under your name.

Alongside the TrueMatix platform, we offer online brand protection as a separate engagement: monitoring marketplaces, social platforms and newly registered domains for listings, accounts and storefronts using your brand without authorisation. Two different problems

Two ways we work.

Platform

Product authentication

Answers one question at the point of purchase: is the item in this customer's hand genuine? Needs a signed code on the packaging and a customer who scans it. Scan data then feeds anomaly and diversion analysis.

Managed service

Online brand protection

Works earlier, before any sale. Looks for the listing, account or domain trading on your name. No code, no packaging change and no customer scan involved — it operates entirely on public online sources.

The two are independent. Many brands take one without the other. Where both run, the only thing they share is your brand and product list.

How it runs

From signal to closed case.

STEP 01

You provide the inputs

Brand and product names, official domains and storefronts, logo and packaging assets, your authorised seller list, and the markets you sell in.

STEP 02

Monitoring runs

Marketplaces, social platforms and new domain registrations are checked continuously against those inputs.

STEP 03

Analyst triage

Every match is reviewed by a person. Authorised resellers and false matches are filtered out before you see anything.

STEP 04

You authorise

Confirmed cases reach you with the evidence attached. Nothing is filed with any platform until you approve that specific report.

STEP 05

Report and follow up

The report goes through the platform's own rights-holder channel. Status is tracked and recurrence is linked back to the original case.

What this does not do

  • Coverage is limited to the platforms in scope for your engagement. No service covers the entire internet, and the specific list is agreed with you before anything starts.
  • Removal is decided by the platform, not by us. We prepare and file the report; the outcome and the timeline are theirs. Takedowns are not guaranteed.
  • A lawful reseller is not an infringement. Selling genuine product you sold them is legal in most markets. Unauthorised distribution is a contractual matter for you, and grey-market diversion is a scan-data question handled by the platform, not here.
  • We are not your legal representative. Reports are filed as your authorised agent through platform processes. Litigation, customs seizure and formal legal notices sit with your counsel.
  • Detection uses public information only. We do not purchase test products, operate undercover accounts or access private data.
  • This service does not verify physical products. A listing being removed says nothing about whether goods already in circulation are genuine.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is this part of the TrueMatix platform?

It is one of the two things TrueMatix offers, but it is not a feature of the authentication software. The platform is software you run on your products; this is a managed service our analysts run for you. They are scoped and priced separately, either works on its own, and cases from this service reach you as reports rather than through the verification dashboard.

What's the difference between a counterfeit and a grey-market product?

A counterfeit was never made by you. A grey-market product is genuine, but sold outside the market or channel it was assigned to. They need different responses: counterfeits are an enforcement matter, diversion is usually a distributor contract matter. This service addresses the first. Diversion is detected through scan data on the platform.

Do I have to change my packaging?

No. This service works entirely from public listings and your brand assets, with no change to the product. The printed code belongs to product authentication, which is the separate platform offering.

Which platforms do you monitor?

Scope is agreed before the engagement starts and depends on where your category actually trades. You receive the specific list of marketplaces, social platforms and domain sources covered. We do not claim access to every platform.

Who authorises a takedown report?

You do. Every confirmed case comes to you with its evidence, and nothing is submitted to a platform until you approve that specific report. We file as your authorised agent, never on our own initiative.

What happens when something is flagged wrongly?

Automated matching produces false positives — an authorised reseller, a brand name that is also a common word, a fan account. That is why an analyst reviews every match before it reaches you. Filtering happens before your queue, not after.

Can a counterfeiter copy the QR code on my product?

A printed code can be photographed and reproduced; any printed mark can. What a copied code cannot do is stay hidden. Each code is unique to one item, so when the same serial is scanned in two places, the duplicate is flagged. That is platform behaviour, not part of this service.
